Dottie Plus

Dottie is free to download, and tracking is free. Dottie Plus is an optional purchase made inside the app, through the App Store.

Plus is sold as a subscription, yearly or monthly, or as a single purchase with no renewal. Apple shows the plans and prices in your region.

Yearly and monthly plans renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends. Subscriptions can be cancelled anytime in your App Store settings.

If the yearly plan offers you a free trial, the first payment is taken when the trial ends, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before then. Dottie sends one reminder two days before a trial ends, if you've allowed notifications.

Apple takes the payment through your Apple Account, and refunds are Apple's to decide. I never see your payment details, and I can't issue a refund myself.

If a subscription ends, nothing you logged or synced is lost. The Plus features pause, and they come back on if you rejoin. Everything that was free stays as it was.

On a new device, or after reinstalling Dottie, tap Restore purchases in Settings, under Dottie Plus. It checks your App Store purchases with Apple. No Dottie account is involved. Plus isn't shared through Family Sharing.
